1. Project Proposal (Appendix A)

Team nameSkyRoute Team
Project titleSkyRoute – An online platform to book tickets from Kabul to beautiful places in the world
Team membersProject Manager, Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Frontend Developer, QA & Documentation (write your real names here)
Problem statementPeople in Kabul often use many agents, phone calls and websites to compare places and prices. This takes time, and prices are not always clear. They need one simple place to find a destination and book a ticket.
Evidence (to collect)Interview 5–10 people (students, families, workers). Ask: How do you book now? What is hard? What would help? Write their answers in Section 2.1.
Target usersTravellers from Kabul (students, families, workers, tourists). Stakeholders: travel agents, airlines, website administrator.
Proposed solutionA responsive website where users explore beautiful places, see prices, book a ticket with a simple form, and manage their trips.
Objectives1) Show destinations with photos and prices. 2) Let users book in under 3 minutes. 3) Let users see and cancel bookings. 4) Work on phone, tablet and desktop.
Scope – includedHome, destinations with search/filter, booking form with validation and price calculator, My Trips, About, Contact, FAQ, videos.
Scope – excludedReal payment, real airline systems, user accounts and a real database (the demo saves bookings in the browser).
Expected benefitsSaves time, clear prices, easy planning, one place for all trips.

2.3 Non-functional requirements

IDRequirement
NFR-01The website must adapt to mobile (360 px), tablet and desktop screens.
NFR-02Main pages should load in under 3 seconds on a normal connection.
NFR-03Every form field must show an error message in plain English when wrong.
NFR-04Navigation must be the same on all pages.
NFR-05Text must be easy to read (dark text on light background, good contrast).
NFR-06The site must work in the latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
NFR-07The code must not contain passwords or private keys.

Assumptions: prices are examples; no real payment. Constraints: bootcamp time; HTML, CSS, JavaScript only.

Cardinality: one User makes many Bookings (1:N). One Destination has many Bookings (1:N). One Category has many Destinations (1:N). One Booking has one or more Passengers (1:N).
